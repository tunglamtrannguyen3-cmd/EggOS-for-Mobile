* 🍴 Fork EggOS.
* 🛠️ Modify the kernel.
* 📱 Port it to new hardware.

# ⚙️ The EggOS Boot Identity

The official EggOS platform may display its attribution during low-level initialization.

A typical startup sequence may look like:

```text
                    POWER ON
                       │
                       ▼
                  🔩 Firmware
                       │
                       ▼
             ┌─────────────────┐
             │      EGGOS      │
             │                 │
             │ Originally      │
             │ created by      │
             │ eggcheese-kun   │
             └─────────────────┘
                       │
                       ▼
                🟢 Host Kernel
                     (Ada)
                       │
                       ▼
                🧠 Virtual Kernel
                       │
                       ▼
                 🖥️ ToT / UI
                       │
                       ▼
               ✨ Userspace ✨
```

The attribution may **hover over the firmware and ToT interfaces** during initialization.

Once userspace takes over:

```text
👋 Attribution overlay → disappears

        ↓

✨ Clean EggOS userspace ✨
```

No permanent watermark.

No giant credit plastered across the desktop.

Just a little reminder during the part of the system where EggOS is establishing its identity. 🥚⚙️
